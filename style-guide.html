Brand

Breakdance Hero is a fast, friendly, mobile-first rhythm game set in a 1980s electro dance battle. The look is neon light on a night-time dancefloor: deep navy and plum fields, hot pink and electric cyan edges, big arcade lettering. It should feel like a block party, not a menu.

Icon. A pink arrow with a cyan outline on --surface with 14/64 corner radius. The arrow is the game's core input and the mark. Source: public/favicon.svg.

Wordmark. “BREAKDANCE” in pink Bungee with a pink glow, “HERO” on the next line in cyan with a cyan glow. Always two lines, always uppercase.

Colour

Neons are accents on dark surfaces. Never put a neon on another neon or use one as a large body-text colour. All tokens live in src/design/tokens.css.

Glow & motion

Glow is the signature effect. Use it on one focal element per screen: the title, the score, the grade or the GO button. Everything else stays flat.

--glow-pink
--glow-cyan
--glow-text-pink

Motion is snappy and on the beat. Meter and state changes take --dur-base (150ms), UI transitions --dur-fast (120ms) with --ease-snap. Honour prefers-reduced-motion for decorative animation; never delay a hit judgement for an effect.

4px base (--space-1 to --space-6). Radius: 8 small, 12 buttons, 14 cards. Respect env(safe-area-inset-*) on notched phones. The game is portrait-first and must also work in short landscape.

Voice

Hype-friendly, short, a little cheeky. Speak like the MC at a block party. Verbs first, no jargon, no guilt when you miss.

Do · “Tap the arrows. Keep the beat.” “Nice windmill!” “Run it back?”
Don't · “You failed.” “Please select a difficulty level to proceed.” Corporate or sarcastic copy.
Do · Use the game's own words: toprock, six-step, windmill, headspin, freeze, b-boy, battle, hype.
Don't · Use real breakers' names, real brands or other people's songs. The music is original.
